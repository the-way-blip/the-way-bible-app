Lamp Bible Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version: Lamp Bible does not collect any information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Lamp Bible is a free Bible reading app for children, made by TheWay in Big Rapids, Michigan.

Information we collect

None. Lamp Bible does not ask for names, email addresses, ages, or any other personal information, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device so a reader can pick up where they left off:

  • reader names you type in (for example, a first name or nickname)
  • each reader's reading level, current chapter, and stars for chapters read
  • settings such as text size, red letters, pictures, and practice sounds

This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Read-aloud

When a reader taps a word or asks the app to read aloud, the app uses the voice built into the device. Lamp Bible does not record or send any audio.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

Lamp Bible has no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no third-party services. It works fully offline.

Children's privacy

Lamp Bible is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
